Gander

A desktop app for reviewing code in the agentic era: agents write the code, the human reviews it.

The mascot is a curious goose with a swept feather tuft, raised eyebrow, and smiling orange bill. The name carries both readings — the bird, and taking a gander at something.

Logo versions

Nine masters in brand/svg/. The color mascot has a cream face and orange bill. Black and white variants use transparent cutouts to preserve the expression in one ink color.

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque, weight 700, letter-spacing -2 at the 92px drawing size. Its strokes pinch and swell the way the goose neck does, which is why it was chosen over the flatter geometric candidates.
