Gander

A desktop app for reviewing code in the agentic era: agents write the code, the human reviews it.

The mark is a Canada goose head in profile, cut from flat geometry. The name carries both readings — the bird, and taking a gander at something.

Logo versions

Nine masters in brand/svg/. The white cheek is negative space rather than a filled shape, so every variant is a single colour and inherits whatever sits behind it.

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque, weight 700, letter-spacing -2 at the 92pt drawing size. Its strokes pinch and swell the way the goose neck does, which is why it was chosen over the flatter geometric candidates.
